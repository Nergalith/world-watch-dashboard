Sources & OSINT Notes

This is a public open-source intelligence (OSINT) aggregator. Nergalith World Watch collects and presents publicly available information from news outlets, official feeds, and open data sources in one place for situational awareness. We do not generate original or proprietary intelligence.

Country View: How This Is Scored

Every hour we collect headlines from the feeds below and tag each one with the countries it names (country names, people's nationality, and major cities or regions, for example Karachi or Balochistan for Pakistan). A headline counts as a conflict report if it uses words like attack, killed, militants, airstrike or fighting. We keep the last 30 days.

Severity reflects how much conflict reporting a country is getting, not casualties or territory. It is the number of conflict reports per day: Red 4 or more, Orange 1.5 or more, Yellow 0.3 or more, Green below that. Grey means none of our sources named the country recently, not that it is calm. Trend compares the last 7 days with the 7 days before (escalating, cooling or steady); it needs 14 days of collection first.

Limits: this is keyword counting, not a human or AI judgement. Countries with more English-language coverage, or with their own outlets in our list (such as Pakistan), get more headlines. Always read the original story.

Curated

Curated Conflict Fallback

If no live public data has been collected, the dashboard shows a small hand-picked set of watch areas, clearly marked "Curated", so the map is never blank. These are not live reports. If live feeds fail after a good run, the last live data is kept and labelled with its age instead.

Planned

Additional Layers

Future layers may include sanctions, advisories, waterways, outages, and verified regional monitoring feeds with clear attribution.

Important

Scope & Limitations

Focus is strictly on conflicts, uprisings, military activity, and related critical geopolitical events. Natural disasters and non-conflict events are deprioritized or excluded. This remains a lightweight public aggregator — not a complete or verified intelligence product.
