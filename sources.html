TRANSPARENCY & METHOD

Sources & OSINT Notes

This is a public open-source intelligence (OSINT) aggregator. Nergalith World Watch collects and presents publicly available information from news outlets, official feeds, and open data sources in one place for situational awareness. We do not generate original or proprietary intelligence.

PUBLIC OSINT AGGREGATOR — This dashboard aggregates publicly available open-source intelligence (OSINT) from news outlets, public feeds, and official sources. We do not generate or claim proprietary intelligence — we simply collect and present it in one easy-to-view location. All items link back to original publishers. Data can be delayed, incomplete, or noisy. Always cross-verify. This is not emergency guidance or classified analysis.

Curated

Curated Conflict Fallback

If no live public data has been collected, the dashboard shows a small hand-picked set of watch areas, clearly marked "Curated", so the map is never blank. These are not live reports. If live feeds fail after a good run, the last live data is kept and labelled with its age instead.

Planned

Additional Layers

Future layers may include sanctions, advisories, waterways, outages, and verified regional monitoring feeds with clear attribution.

Important

Scope & Limitations

Focus is strictly on conflicts, uprisings, military activity, and related critical geopolitical events. Natural disasters and non-conflict events are deprioritized or excluded. This remains a lightweight public aggregator — not a complete or verified intelligence product.
