How The Map Stays Current

Sources And Notes

World Conflict Tracking combines public reporting signals, open RSS headlines, and natural event data. Items remain linked to their publishers so readers can verify the original reporting.

USGS Earthquakes

Earthquake markers use the U.S. Geological Survey GeoJSON feed for magnitude 4.5+ events recorded in the past seven days.

Curated Conflict Fallback

When public feeds fail or rate-limit, the map falls back to globally relevant watch areas instead of going empty.

Additional Layers

Future layers may include sanctions, advisories, waterways, outages, and verified regional monitoring feeds with clear attribution.

Note

Public Data Disclaimer

Open-source feeds can be delayed, duplicated, incomplete, or noisy. World Conflict Tracking is situational awareness, not emergency guidance.
