How World Watch Works

Sources And Notes

World Watch uses open public signals where possible and clearly labels demo layers. It is built for fast scanning, source discovery, and basic situational awareness.

GDELT Public Event Signals

Global news geography signals for conflict, politics, unrest, maritime disruption, cyber reporting, and infrastructure topics.

Curated OSINT Fallback

When public feeds fail or rate-limit, the dashboard falls back to a small curated set of globally relevant watch areas.

Demo

Maritime Context

Ships and route overlays show strategic lanes, chokepoints, and disruption zones without overloading the public page.

Demo

Aviation Context

Aircraft layers represent public watch areas and air corridors so visitors can understand why a region matters.

Planned

Public Advisories

Future public layers can include government advisories, maritime notices, cyber alerts, and major infrastructure warnings.

Note

Public Data Disclaimer

Open-source feeds can be delayed, duplicated, incomplete, or noisy. World Watch is situational awareness, not emergency guidance.
